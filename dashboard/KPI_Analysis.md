# KPI Analysis — Sales Dashboard

## 1. Total Sales

Measures the total revenue generated from all sales transactions.

Formula: Sum of all Sales values.

## 2. Total Profit

Measures the total profit earned after accounting for losses.

Formula: Sum of all Profit values.

## 3. Units Sold

Measures the total quantity of products sold.

Formula: Sum of all Quantity values.

## 4. Profit Margin

Measures profit as a percentage of total sales.

Formula: (Total Profit / Total Sales) * 100

## Planned Visualizations

* Monthly Sales Trend — Line Chart
* Sales by Region — Bar Chart
* Sales by Category — Column Chart

## Planned Filters

* Region
* Month

## Purpose

These KPIs help users understand business performance and compare sales across regions, products, and time periods.
